Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy explains what Take Her Out collects, why, and who we share it with.

Information we collect

  • Account: your email address.
  • Preferences: your sign, your partner's sign, relationship type, distance, budget preference, city, and ZIP or postal code.
  • Activity: the actions you choose to rewrite, stored for a short time.
  • Payments: Stripe handles payment details. We receive your subscription status and a Stripe customer ID. We don't store card numbers.

How we use it

We use your information to sign you in, generate daily insights and suggestions, find nearby events based on your city, process subscriptions, and keep the Service secure.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase: database and sign-in.
  • Stripe: payments and subscriptions.
  • Netlify: hosting and app functions.
  • OpenRouter (AI model provider): your signs, relationship details, city, and the action text are sent to generate suggestions.
  • FindLocal: your city is used to look up events.
  • Photon (Komoot) and OpenStreetMap: city search when you type your location.
  • Google Fonts: loads fonts when you visit.

We don't sell your personal information.

Cookies and browser storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in. We don't use advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

Rewritten actions are deleted automatically within about two days. Account and preference data is kept while your account is active, and we delete it when you ask us to.
